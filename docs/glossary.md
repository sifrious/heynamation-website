# Glossary

- Company information page. The public, read-only page identifying Heynamation, LLC. Defined in `routes/web.php` and rendered by `resources/views/company.blade.php`.
- Website privacy notice. The description of this website's data handling in `resources/views/company.blade.php`. It does not describe other company products or certify legal compliance.
- Health check. The `/up` endpoint registered in `bootstrap/app.php`. It reports whether Laravel can boot, not whether external services are healthy.
